# Problem Statement

Accommodation discovery is fragmented, stale, difficult to compare, and vulnerable to inaccurate or fraudulent listings.

## Problems to solve
- Finding relevant homes, PGs, and rooms.
- Comparing true monthly cost and amenities.
- Knowing whether a listing is genuine and available.
- Communicating with providers efficiently.
- Moving from discovery to viewing and eventually tenancy.

## Non-goals initially
- Full property-management SaaS.
- Complex financial products.
- Short-stay hospitality as the primary market.
