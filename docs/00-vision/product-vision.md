# myStay Product Vision

## Vision
myStay is a trusted long-term accommodation platform for discovering, evaluating, securing, and managing where people live.

## Initial Focus
- Homes and apartments
- PGs
- Private rooms
- Shared rooms
- Co-living

## North Star
Help a seeker move from discovery to a suitable, trustworthy place to live with the least uncertainty and friction.

## Principles
1. Trust before growth.
2. Discovery before transactions.
3. Accurate availability matters.
4. One unified accommodation model.
5. Build modularly for long-term expansion.
