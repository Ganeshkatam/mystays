# myStay MVP Scope

## Objective
Build a long-term accommodation discovery marketplace for homes, PGs, private rooms, and shared rooms.

## In scope
- Seeker registration, login, profile, and preferences
- Provider onboarding and basic profile
- Property and inventory creation
- Listing creation, editing, publishing, pausing, and archiving
- Listing photos/media
- Location and map-based discovery
- Search, filters, sorting, pagination
- Budget, accommodation type, furnishing, amenities, occupancy, and availability filters
- Listing detail pages
- Favorites
- Saved searches
- Listing inquiry/contact workflow
- Basic provider lead management
- Listing freshness/availability controls
- User/listing reporting
- Admin moderation and basic verification
- Basic analytics and audit logging

## Out of scope for MVP
- Online rent/payment collection
- Binding booking transactions
- Rental agreements/e-signatures
- Maintenance management
- Full resident/tenant portal
- Roommate matching
- AI recommendations
- Provider SaaS billing
- Short-stay/hotel workflows

## MVP success
A seeker can reliably discover a suitable long-term listing, understand the important terms, save it, and contact the provider; a provider can publish accurate inventory and respond to demand.
